✦ MidLink Community Rules

Please follow these rules to help keep every deal safe, fair, and respectful.

  1. Be respectful. Treat buyers, sellers, and community members kindly.
  2. Keep deals transparent. Agree on the item, price, and terms before making a payment.
  3. Use a trusted middleman. For eligible deals, use the platform’s middleman process and verify the details in the official channel.
  4. Keep communication on-platform. Avoid moving a deal to unofficial chats where it cannot be reviewed or supported.
  5. Protect your account. Never share your password, verification codes, or private account details.
  6. Report suspicious activity. Contact support if someone pressures you, changes agreed terms, or asks you to bypass the process.
